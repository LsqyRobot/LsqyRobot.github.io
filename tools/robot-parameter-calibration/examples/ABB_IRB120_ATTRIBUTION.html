ABB IRB 120 example attribution

abb_irb120_3_58.urdf is a kinematics-only expanded adaptation of
ROS-Industrial’s abb_irb120_support/urdf/irb120_3_58_macro.xacro:

https://github.com/ros-industrial/abb/blob/noetic/abb_irb120_support/urdf/irb120_3_58_macro.xacro

The upstream package is licensed under Apache-2.0. Its package.xml states
that the joint limits and maximum velocities are based on ABB’s IRB 120
technical data sheet and asks users to check the configuration against their
particular robot:

https://github.com/ros-industrial/abb/blob/noetic/abb_irb120_support/package.xml

ABB’s product specification identifies the IRB 120 as a 3 kg robot with
0.58 m reach:

https://library.e.abb.com/public/429d251dbce44b049ba7d6107a422de0/3HAC035960 PS IRB 120-en.pdf

The full license text distributed with this fixture is in
ABB_IRB120_LICENSE.txt.

Robotics Workbench made the following changes to the upstream Xacro:

  • expanded the empty ${prefix} and ${radians(90)} expressions;
  • removed Xacro/material definitions plus visual, collision and inertial data;
  • removed the auxiliary fixed base_link → base branch;
  • reparented tool0 from link_6 to the identity-coincident flange and
    renamed that fixed joint from link_6-tool0 to flange-tool0;
  • changed the robot name to identify this as a kinematics-only demo.

The six motion-joint origins, axes and default limits are unchanged. The net
link_6 → flange and link_6 → tool0 transforms are also unchanged. The
machine-readable test inputs and acceptance thresholds are recorded in
abb_irb120_demo.json.
